2. The cost of memoryHarga memori

An entity with honest history absorbs abuse before feeling anything. That is the point of having a memory — and also its cost, because the same history shields an attacker who earned it. The question is how large the shield gets, and whether it saturates.Entitas dengan riwayat jujur menyerap penyalahgunaan sebelum merasakan apa pun. Itu tujuan punya memori — dan juga harganya, karena riwayat yang sama melindungi penyerang yang mendapatkannya. Pertanyaannya seberapa besar tamengnya, dan apakah ia jenuh.

3. The intake discount against farmingDiskon intake terhadap farming

Farming means earning trust through volume. The answer is not a decision-layer ceiling — D49 showed those arrive too late — but pricing each positive at intake by the shape of the gaps it arrived in. Bursty human-shaped activity earns full credit; perfectly regular intervals do not.Farming berarti mengumpulkan trust lewat volume. Jawabannya bukan ceiling di lapis keputusan — D49 menunjukkan itu datang terlambat — tapi menghargai setiap positive saat intake menurut bentuk jeda kedatangannya. Aktivitas bursty berbentuk manusia mendapat kredit penuh; interval sempurna teratur tidak.

8. What traffic falsifiedApa yang difalsifikasi trafik

Traffic has overturned 8 claims so far. Each correction is visible in the design record; listing them here is the site keeping itself honest about what changed and why.Trafik sudah membalikkan 8 klaim sejauh ini. Setiap koreksi terlihat di rekaman desain; mendaftarkannya di sini adalah cara situs menjaga kejujurannya tentang apa yang berubah dan kenapa.

D46 (personas)
The developing stage ceiling was one rung too high. Two mistyped PINs and an autofilled password manager were all advised INCREASE_FRICTION. The middle stage is meant to let trust influence a treatment without driving it, and OBSERVE is the only rung that does that.Ceiling tahap developing satu tingkat terlalu tinggi. Dua PIN salah ketik dan satu password manager yang autofill semuanya disarankan INCREASE_FRICTION. Tahap tengah seharusnya membiarkan trust memengaruhi perlakuan tanpa menentukannya, dan OBSERVE satu-satunya tingkat yang begitu.
D46 (personas)
Scope entropy was normalised against the window size, which made it measure how many scopes the application has rather than how varied the entity was. A two-scope app could not reach the diversity threshold at all, so its most honest user scored monotonous for an entire run.Entropi scope dinormalisasi terhadap ukuran window, sehingga yang terukur adalah berapa banyak scope yang dimiliki aplikasi, bukan seberapa beragam entitas-nya. Aplikasi dua scope tak bisa mencapai ambang diversity sama sekali, jadi penggunanya yang paling jujur terbaca monoton sepanjang run.
D48 (personas)
A hard declaration that was not provable. “Paid work requires enough credits” looks provable and is not: the client’s view of its balance is stale by construction. Being enforced by the server is not the same as being provable, so it is soft now.Sebuah deklarasi hard yang ternyata tak terbuktikan. “Pekerjaan berbayar butuh kredit cukup” tampak terbuktikan tapi tidak: pandangan klien atas saldonya kedaluwarsa secara konstruksi. Ditegakkan server tidak sama dengan bisa dibuktikan, jadi sekarang ia soft.
D49 (personas)
The saturation guard did not do what it claimed. Farming produces a high mean, a high mean proposes ALLOW, and a ceiling can only lower a decision — so the D37 gate was unobservable for exactly the entity it targeted. Measured: 300 uniform positives absorbed 17 strong negatives before the advice moved.Saturation guard tidak melakukan apa yang diklaimnya. Farming menghasilkan mean tinggi, mean tinggi mengusulkan ALLOW, dan ceiling hanya bisa menurunkan keputusan — jadi gate D37 tak teramati justru bagi entitas yang ia sasar. Terukur: 300 positive seragam menyerap 17 negative kuat sebelum sarannya bergerak.
D57 (live)
A 200 is not a success. The origin answers unknown /api/* paths with its SPA fallback, so a path scanner asking for /api/admin/users was credited with a positive and ended with more trust than it started with. An API route answering a document is a 404 the origin failed to say.Status 200 bukan berarti sukses. Origin menjawab path /api/* yang tak dikenal dengan fallback SPA-nya, jadi pemindai path yang meminta /api/admin/users justru dikreditkan positive dan berakhir dengan trust lebih tinggi daripada saat mulai. Route API yang menjawab dokumen adalah 404 yang gagal diucapkan origin.
D57 (live)
One scope for every page reproduced the entropy bug in a new place. All page views shared a single scope, so scopeEntropy was 0 and distinctScopes was 1 for every visitor who only browsed: honest readers scored monotonous by construction. The feature was measuring the integration’s coarseness, not the visitor.Satu scope untuk semua halaman mengulang bug entropi di tempat baru. Semua page view memakai satu scope, jadi scopeEntropy 0 dan distinctScopes 1 untuk setiap pengunjung yang cuma membaca: pembaca jujur terbaca monoton secara konstruksi. Fiturnya mengukur kekasaran integrasinya, bukan pengunjungnya.
D57 (live)
Weak signals on page views made the operator’s own uptime check a false positive — a 20-request monitor reached INCREASE_FRICTION at step 15. Over HTTP alone an uptime monitor and a crawler are the same client. Signals are attached only to actions now, and the cost is stated rather than hidden: a crawler that only reads public pages walks through at ALLOW.Weak signal pada page view membuat uptime check operatornya sendiri jadi false positive — monitor 20 request mencapai INCREASE_FRICTION di langkah 15. Lewat HTTP saja, uptime monitor dan crawler adalah klien yang sama. Sinyal sekarang hanya menempel pada aksi, dan biayanya dinyatakan bukan disembunyikan: crawler yang cuma membaca halaman publik lolos di ALLOW.
D57 (live)
Two harness bugs that would have read as model behavior. scrollIntoView and getBoundingClientRect in one expression yields pre-scroll coordinates, so every form click landed on empty space and looked like “the persona did not convert”. And an unbounded protocol command hung a whole run on one unreachable redirect.Dua bug harness yang akan terbaca sebagai perilaku model. scrollIntoView dan getBoundingClientRect dalam satu ekspresi menghasilkan koordinat sebelum scroll, jadi setiap klik form mendarat di ruang kosong dan tampak seperti “persona-nya tidak konversi”. Dan satu perintah protokol tanpa batas waktu menggantung seluruh run karena satu redirect yang tak terjangkau.
