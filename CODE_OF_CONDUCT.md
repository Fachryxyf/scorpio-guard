# Code of Conduct

A small project about one narrow subject: whether a trust model is right. Disagreement
about the model is the useful kind of traffic here.

## What is expected

- Argue about the decision, the evidence, or the code, and say which of the three you mean.
- Assume the other person has read less of the record than you have, and link the entry
  instead of restating it.
- Keep a report about behaviour out of the issue tracker. SECURITY.md names a private form
  that reaches one person.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, sustained disruption, publishing someone else private
information, or reopening a closed decision under a new name in order to relitigate it. A
maintainer may edit, lock, or close a thread for any of these, and says which one.

## Scope and enforcement

It applies to issues, pull requests, and anywhere someone is representing the project. There
is one maintainer, so enforcement is one person reading and deciding, with the reason given.
No ombudsman and no committee is claimed, because there is not one.
